02 / WHAT WE’RE BUILDING — IN DEVELOPMENT

Repeatable tests of AI tools on real tasks.

We’re developing repeatable tests of AI tools, starting with AI app builders. Each comparison will use the same tasks across several tools, with multiple runs to examine how results vary. We’ll combine automated tests with hands-on review to examine both the output and the experience of using each tool.

The aim is to bring these comparisons together with ValueCompass, so you can consider practical fit and values in the same decision.

WHAT EACH COMPARISON WILL EXAMINE

RESULTS

Does it work?

Does the output do what the task asked?

FIXES

What needs fixing?

What errors occur, and how many attempts does it take to resolve them?

TIME

How long does it take?

Time from the initial instructions to a usable result.

COST

What does it cost?

The cost of completing the task, including retries.

ASSISTANCE

How much help does it need?

The guidance and technical knowledge needed to reach a useful result.

03 / HOW WE APPROACH A CHOICE

Three questions worth asking of any AI tool.

TASK

Can it do the task?

A tool’s strengths need to match the work you want to do.

EXPERIENCE

What is it like to use?

The setup and effort involved need to suit your skills and how you work.

VALUES

Does it fit what matters to you?

Your choice also supports the companies behind the tools, with their ownership, practices and commitments.
